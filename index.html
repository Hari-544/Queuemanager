How it works

How the demo works

Follow these steps to try the full citizen-to-staff flow.

  1. Open the citizen page and choose an office and service.
  2. Pick a date and an available time slot.
  3. Receive a unique token and reference.
  4. Open the staff demo.
  5. Call the token and change its status.
  6. Complete the appointment.
  7. Watch the dashboard counts update.

QueueEase - Less waiting. Better service.

QueueEase is a Community Service Internship (CSI) demonstration for public-service queue management. No real-provider pilot or measured impact is claimed.

Demo data is stored only in this browser (local storage) and does not sync between users or devices.

A student prototype. Not affiliated with, endorsed or approved by any government department.
